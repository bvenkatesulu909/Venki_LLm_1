What this is

Venki_llm_1 is a real decoder-only transformer implemented by hand — no transformers or tiktoken shortcuts. Every component (BPE tokenizer, causal multi-head attention, training loop, 2-bit quantizer) is written from scratch and trained on ~1.9M tokens of public-domain literature. The checkpoint is compressed to 2 bits and runs inference here in your browser via a ported JS model (see build-docs below).
